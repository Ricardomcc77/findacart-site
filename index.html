A few useful details

Before you ask.

Can I download FindACart yet?

Not yet. FindACart is in development for the web, iPhone, and Android. We’ll publish availability here when the app is ready. There is no public download or signup at this time.

Can I order groceries through FindACart?

Not today. FindACart is for planning meals and organizing shopping lists. Grocery ordering and delivery are not currently available.

Where can I find company and privacy information?

FindACart is built by FindACart LLC. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Company email: support@findacart.com.
