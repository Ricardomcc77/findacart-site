Introduction

FindACart ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website (findacart.com), mobile application, and browser extension (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password when you register; you may also choose to add an optional phone number and postal address to your profile
  • Profile Data: Dietary preferences, food allergies, household size
  • User Content: Shopping lists, saved recipes, meal plans you create
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team

Sensitive information: We do not collect government identifiers or payment credentials. Dietary preferences and allergy information you choose to provide could relate to health or religious practice. We use it solely to filter recipes and meal plans — never for advertising, for profiling beyond providing the Service, or for sale.

Third-Party Sign-In (Google OAuth)

You may choose to sign in using your Google account. When you do, we receive:

  • Email Address: Your Google account email
  • Name: Your first and last name from your Google profile
  • Profile Picture: Your Google profile photo (if available)

How we use this data: We use this information solely for authentication and to create/link your FindACart account. We do not access your Google contacts, calendar, files, or any other Google services. We do not share your Google data with third parties except as described in this policy.

Revoking access: You can disconnect Google sign-in at any time from your Google Account permissions. This does not delete your FindACart account; you can still sign in with email/password.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on pages
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, referring URLs
  • Cookies: Session cookies for authentication, preference cookies

Voice Input (Optional)

Adding items to a shopping list by voice is optional and uses your browser's or device's built-in speech recognition. That speech service turns your speech into text, and only the resulting text is used to add items. Depending on your browser or device, the service may process the audio off-device: for example, Chrome sends audio to Google, and in the FindACart iPhone app, Apple's speech recognition may send your audio to Apple's servers to recognize it. Those providers handle the audio under their own privacy terms. FindACart does not record or store your voice audio and never sends it to our servers. We do not keep the recognized transcript; we keep only the shopping-list items you add. The microphone is used only while you are actively dictating an item. In the iPhone app you can turn off FindACart's access to the microphone and to speech recognition at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under FindACart. When AI parsing is enabled, FindACart sends the recognized text and speech locale to its configured provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, to identify shopping items. We do not send your account identifiers or existing list contents to that provider. Provider retention policies apply; this is separate from the browser or device speech service.

Cashback Program Data

Cashback is coming soon and is not yet available. The practices below describe how the program will work once it launches.

  • Click Tracking: When you activate cashback, we record the store, timestamp, and your user ID
  • Transaction Data: Our partners may share purchase confirmation and amounts for cashback calculation (we do not see your payment details)
  • Fraud Prevention Data: Cashback activations and reward redemptions record your IP address and browser/device information to prevent fraud and abuse. This information is kept with your cashback records and is deleted with them when you delete your account (see Data Retention below).

Referral Program Data

The referral program is not yet available. The practices below describe how it will work once it launches.

  • Referral Attribution: Every member gets a personal referral link. If you sign up through a friend's link or enter their referral code, we store which member referred you (the attribution) so that any reward can be correctly credited. We keep the referral link between the two accounts, the reward's status, and the dates it moved through.
  • Referral Rewards: If a friend you referred subscribes, we record the resulting reward — a Premium Pass — on your account (its status, grant date, and expiry). A Premium Pass is FindACart Premium access only; it has no cash value and is never paid out as money or a gift card.

What the referrer sees: As a referrer, you only ever see counts — how many referrals are pending and how many rewards you have earned — plus an approximate date your next reward is expected. You never see who your friends are, their email, their plan, or the date any individual friend paid. Our support staff can view the referral link between two accounts to answer support questions and prevent fraud, as part of normal internal account administration. We do not share referral attribution data with any third party.

Browser Extension Data

Cashback earnings are coming soon. If you install the extension, the store-detection and local-processing practices below apply now; earning and activation apply after launch.

  • Local URL Matching: To find supported cashback stores, the extension processes URLs from the tabs you visit locally and compares their domains with its cached partner-store list
  • Partner Store Visits: When that local match finds a supported cashback store, the extension can show you a cashback notification
  • Activation Events: When you click "Activate cashback," we record this to track your rewards
  • Authentication Tokens: Stored locally in your browser to keep you signed in
  • Partner-Domain State: The only state retained from a tab visit is a matched partner domain, used for notification cooldowns and active cashback sessions

What We DON'T Collect: We do not transmit or retain non-partner URLs, or use them to build a browsing history. The extension only activates on matched partner cashback stores. We never see your passwords, credit card numbers, or purchase details.

  • Personalization: Customize recipe recommendations based on your preferences

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

Service Providers

  • Our cashback partner: User ID and click data to track and attribute cashback rewards (once the cashback program launches)
  • Spoonacular (Recipe API): Search queries and the recipe filters you set — which can include your dietary preferences, allergies, and excluded ingredients — are sent without your name, email, or account identifiers to provide recipe data. Recipe images load in your browser or app directly from Spoonacular's servers, so Spoonacular receives your IP address when images render, as with any image host.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central (Barcode Lookups): When you scan a product barcode, we send only the scanned barcode — no personal identifiers — to look up product details
  • Tremendous (Gift Cards): Name and email to deliver gift card rewards when you redeem. Once you redeem a reward and interact with Tremendous directly, Tremendous handles the data you provide to it as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloud Hosting Provider: All data is stored on secure cloud servers
  • Brevo (Email Service): Email address and name to send transactional and marketing emails
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): If you turn on push notifications, your device's push token — an anonymous delivery handle, not your name, email, or list contents — is sent to Google to deliver shared-list updates and account notifications (such as a household ownership change or a referral reward) to that device. Each notification also carries routing details: its type, an internal notification number and, for a shared-list update, an internal list identifier. Where push notifications are available and turned on, they can reach Android, the web and the iPhone app. On iPhone, Google hands the notification to Apple's push service (Apple Push Notification service) to deliver it, so the push token, the notification text and these routing details also pass through Apple. You can turn push notifications off at any time in your device or browser settings.
  • Apple (Speech Recognition): If you use voice input in the FindACart iPhone app, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, and Apple may process the audio on its servers to do so. FindACart receives only the resulting text and keeps only the shopping-list items you add.
  • PostHog (Product Analytics): We use PostHog to understand how the Service is used so we can improve it. We send PostHog usage events — for example, a page or screen viewed, an item added to a shopping list, or a meal plan generated. When you are signed in, these events are linked to an internal account number only, never your name or email. When you are signed out, events are anonymous: on the website the anonymous analytics identifier is kept in memory only and does not persist across page loads (no analytics cookies); in the mobile app it is stored on your device. Along with each event, PostHog also receives standard technical details — your device type and model, operating system and browser or app version, and your IP address, which PostHog uses to derive an approximate (city-level) location. We do not send PostHog the contents of your lists, recipes, or voice input, and we never use analytics data for advertising. We also use PostHog for error tracking: when the app hits an unexpected error, we send the error type and the technical stack trace so we can diagnose and fix it. We strip the error message text before sending so it cannot carry anything you typed, and errors reported by our servers are not linked to your account. When you delete your account, we also delete your analytics data held by PostHog.

Recall alerts: We check the items on your shopping lists and in your meal plans against official FDA and FSIS recall feeds, by product name, to flag possible recall matches. In the planner, only meals made from recipes you wrote yourself or imported from a link carry ingredients we can check; meals from the recipe catalog and saved catalog recipes are checked once their ingredients are on a shopping list, and title-only meals are covered only through your shopping list. So that a recall announced after your trip can still reach food you may have at home, when an item on a list you own is checked off (by you or by someone you share the list with) we keep a small purchase record: the item's name, the list it was on, and when it was last checked off. It does not record who checked it off, quantities, prices, or stores. The record stays when you clear or delete the item, is used only for these recall checks, and is included in your data export. Matching is name-based, not UPC- or lot-verified, so an alert means you may have the item, not that you bought a recalled product. We use this only to alert you — we never sell or profile your shopping data.

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account Data: Until you delete your account
  • Recall Purchase Records: Kept for three months after the item was last checked off, then removed by a daily cleanup; deleted sooner when you delete that list or your account. Recall alerts in your in-app alert history stay there until you delete your account.
  • Cashback Records: Until you delete your account. Your cashback clicks, earnings, and reward redemptions (including the fraud-prevention data kept with them) are deleted with your account.
  • Usage Logs: 90 days
  • Deleted Accounts: Data purged within 30 days, except where legally required and except the administrative audit records described below
  • Administrative Audit Records: If your account was ever the subject of an administrative action (for example, a moderation decision such as a suspension) or of staff access to your account data (for example, a support agent viewing your account to assist you), we keep a minimal, append-only record of that event after your account is deleted. That record contains an internal account identifier, the action taken or access made, its outcome, any change to your account role, and any reason our staff recorded. To hold our staff accountable, the record also stores details of the staff member and the request that performed the action, such as the acting staff member's identifier, IP address, and device information; that data identifies our administrator, not you. We keep these records to preserve the integrity of our moderation history, to hold our staff accountable for access to customer data, and to prevent fraud and abuse

Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Bcrypt hashing for passwords (we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Opaque tokens with automatic rotation for authentication
  • Regular security audits and monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Household Sharing Privacy

Our household sharing feature allows you to share shopping lists and meal plans with family members. This section explains how your data is handled when using this feature.

What Household Members Can See

When you share content with your household, members can see:

  • Shared Shopping Lists: List name, items, quantities, and check-off status
  • Shared Meal Plans: Recipes assigned to each day of shared weeks
  • Your Name: Your first name, shown next to items you check off on a shared list. A checked-off item keeps your first name until it is unchecked, even if you later leave the household.
  • Household Name: The name you chose for your household
  • Household Meal Safety Settings: A shared list of intolerances (e.g. peanut, shellfish) that a household owner or admin configures for the household. These settings are entered explicitly for the household and are used when generating household meal plans — they are not copied from, and do not reveal, any member's private food preferences.

Everyone in your household can see the household member list, which shows each member's first and last name and household role. The household owner and admins can also see each member's email address so they can manage the household; we do not send your email address to other members.

What Remains Private

The following data is never shared with household members:

  • Your password
  • Your email address, except that the household owner and admins can see it (see above)
  • Your cashback earnings and redemption history
  • Shopping lists and meal plans you haven't explicitly shared
  • Your saved recipes and recipe collections
  • Your personal dietary preferences and allergies (these are never shared with household members and are never auto-added to the shared Household Meal Safety Settings)
  • Your account settings and notification preferences

How Sharing Works

  • Opt-In Only: Content is private by default. You must explicitly click "Share" to make a list or meal plan visible to household members.
  • Revocable: You can unshare content at any time by clicking the share button again.
  • Household-Scoped: Shared content is only visible to members of your household, not to the public.

Sharing One List With One Person

If you accept an invitation to someone else's shopping list, that list's owner can see your first name in the list's sharing settings. When that invitation is your only access to the list, items you check off on it are shown as checked by 'External collaborator', not by your name. If you can also reach the list through your household, your check-offs show your first name, as described above.

Leaving a Household

You can leave a household at any time from Account > Household. When you leave:

  • You immediately lose access to content shared with that household. A list someone shared with you individually stays available until that share ends.
  • Content you created and shared reverts to your personal account
  • Other members lose access to content you had shared

Households and Children

Household sharing links separate FindACart accounts, and every member joins with their own account, so all household members must be 18 or older. We do not offer child or managed sub-accounts, and minors should not create an account or join a household. For family meal planning, use your own adult account.

Household Data Deletion

If you delete your account and you own a household with other active members, ownership automatically transfers to a remaining member so the household stays available. The household is deleted only if you are its last active member.
